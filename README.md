# Don't Look Away

You are lost in a dark forest and must collect all the pages to uncover the story and survive. 

---

## 📥 Installation

### **Windows**
1. Download the `.zip` file  
2. Extract it anywhere  
3. Run **DontLookAway.exe**

### **macOS**
1. Download the archive  
2. Open it  
3. Run the **DontLookAway** application  
   - If macOS blocks it, right‑click → *Open* → confirm

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **WASD** — Move  
- **Mouse** — Look around  
- **E** — Pick up pages, and any other interactable objects
- **Shift** - Run
- **Esp** - Pause
- **Q** - Quit game (All Progess Is Lost)
###HEALTH
- Yes you have health, how much? how little? only one way to find out

## 📘 Complete Strategy Guide

Not a whole lot of tips are given within the game itself. We, the developers, want the players to be able to figure out things for themselves and uncover all the secrets in the game. That said, it can be difficult, so if you would rather, we have typed up this guide to provide you with a comprehensive look at the mechanics of the game.

### 🔊 Heartbeat Awareness
- Listen for the heartbeat. This is your best method of avoiding the enemies.
- If you hear one, it’s already really close to you, but the heartbeat will get faster the closer it gets.

---

### 🗺️ Map & Item Behavior
- Don’t bother memorizing your way around the map. Every game generates a new map, so you won’t be able to rely on your knowledge of where things are from previous playthroughs.
- That said, the items are always hidden in the same spots. If you recognize a building or object from before, there is likely a page there too, if you haven’t found it.

---

### 🪧 Signposts
- The signposts are your main guide to finding the pages.
- They glow when there is still a page in that direction to find.
- The glow is gone if you’ve already found that page.

---

### 📄 Page Effects & Difficulty Changes
- Most of the time, the items you find do not have a direct effect on the gameplay.
- **Page 2** generates an enemy right next to you as soon as you put the page down. Be prepared to dodge this attack before you put down the page.
- After **page 5**, the difficulty in the game changes:
  - The enemies become faster  
  - There are more of them  
  - They gain the ability to teleport

---

### 👁️ Enemy Avoidance & Staring Mechanics
- Staring at the enemies is your main defense in the game.
- The best strategy is to look at them and back up to where you are going until you have enough distance between you to turn around.

#### After Page 5
- Staring at your enemies is no longer an option.
- If you look at them for too long, they will teleport behind you and attack you.
- Now you must switch your strategy to constantly rotating:
  - Look at them and back up  
  - Look away for a second  
  - Look back at them again  
- As long as you look away every couple seconds, they won’t teleport.

---

### 🏃 Movement & Sprinting
- Always stay moving. The enemies are faster than you.
- Your sprint can be a life saver sometimes, but it doesn’t last very long.
- Best to wait until there are enemies near before using it.

---

### 📜 Reading Pages Safely
- When you collect a page, it automatically comes up on the screen, but the game does not pause.
- Press **Escape twice** to put the page down and then pull it up again to pause the game so you can read it.
- Save for the final page — you will have to read this one while running.

---

### 📼 Cassette Tapes
- While we recommend picking up any cassette tapes you find as it adds to the story, they are not necessary to complete the game.

---

### ❤️ Health & Damage
- You can be hit **three times** before you die.
- There are a couple of health packs around which will let you be hit an additional time, but they are very rare, so don’t rely on them.

---

### 🔑 Keys & Locked House
- One page is hidden in a house that requires a key.
- There are a couple keys hidden throughout the map; each one will open the door to this house.

---

### 🏁 Endings
- The ending written on the seventh page is not the true ending.
- Beat the game on **Normal Mode** to unlock **Hard Mode**.
- In Hard Mode, you will find a page containing the true ending.
